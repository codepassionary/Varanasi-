Interactive Yatra Itinerary

Nov 20 – Nov 26, 2026 Schedule & Highlights

D1

Nov 20: Departure from Rewa

Boarding Rewa-Bilaspur Express (18:00 IST)

Transit

Assemble at Rewa Junction station. Final check of spiritual items and travel essentials before train departure at 18:00 towards Satna Junction.

D2

Nov 21: Arrival in Holy Kashi & Ghat Check-in

Ganga Snan & Dashashwamedh Evening Aarti

Spiritual

Arrive in Varanasi Junction early morning. Hotel check-in near Ghat area. Evening holy dip at Ganges and attending the grand Dashashwamedh Ghat Ganga Aarti.

D3

Nov 22: Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Darshan

★ Core Event: Golden Temple & Corridor Experience

Key Highlight

Early morning Sugam Darshan at Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga Dham. Exploration of Vishwanath Corridor, Annapurna Temple, and Kaal Bhairav Darshan (The Kotwal of Kashi).

D4

Nov 23: Sarnath Pilgrimage & Assi Ghat Morning

Subah-e-Banaras & Buddhist Heritage Site

Spiritual

Experience "Subah-e-Banaras" at Assi Ghat with classical music and yoga. Afternoon excursion to Sarnath (Dhamek Stupa & Archaeological Museum).

D5

Nov 24: Dev Deepawali Grand Light Night

★ Grand Event: 100,000+ Diyas & Special Boat Cruise

Dev Deepawali

The crown jewel of Kashi Yatra! Evening boat ride along the Ganges to view all 84 Ghats illuminated by millions of earthen lamps, accompanied by laser lights and fireworks.

D6-7

Nov 25–26: Farewell Kashi & Return Journey

Boarding Mahanagari Express & Arrival in Rewa

Transit

Morning local shopping for Banarasi Silk and souvenirs. Evening departure on Mahanagari Express (22:30 IST). Arrival in Rewa on Nov 26 afternoon.
